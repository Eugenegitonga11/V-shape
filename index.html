    V-Shape is Vivace’s non-surgical body contouring programme. It brings together Onda Pro for stubborn fat, cellulite and loose skin, and Sculptix and Wonder for muscle toning, so your plan can target fat, skin and muscle together.

    No. Body sculpting reshapes specific areas rather than reducing overall body weight. It works best for stubborn pockets of fat and areas that need toning once you are close to your goal weight. For medical weight loss, ask about our V-Slender programme.

    Onda Pro feels like a warm massage, with built-in cooling to keep the skin comfortable. Muscle sculpting with Sculptix or Wonder feels like an intense workout: strong muscle contractions, with some muscle tiredness afterwards, as you would feel after the gym.

    Generally none. You can go back to work or your normal routine straight after your session. Some people notice mild redness after Onda Pro or muscle soreness after muscle sculpting, which settles on its own.

    Most people need a course of sessions to see their best results, and results build gradually over the following weeks. Your plan depends on the area and your goals, and we will recommend a course at your consultation.

    Body sculpting is not suitable during pregnancy, or for people with a pacemaker, metal implants in the treatment area or certain medical conditions. Our team will go through your medical history before you start.
